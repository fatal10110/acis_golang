# NNNN. Title in the form of a decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Sources:** links to the plan, issue or pull request

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces, constraints and facts matter? Give the numbers if there
are any. Write for a reader who was not there.

## Decision

What we do, in one or two short paragraphs. Use active voice: "We run ...", "Each actor has ...".

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and the costs we accept. Include the rules contributors
must now follow, and the tests or checks that keep the decision in place.

## Alternatives considered

Each option we did not take, with the reason in a sentence or two.
